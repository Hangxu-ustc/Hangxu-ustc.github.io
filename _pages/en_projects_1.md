The first systematic piece in my graduate study. Segment Anything performs impressively on natural images but degrades noticeably on remote-sensing and medical domains. The survey asks two questions: where does the degradation come from, and how can transfer be done.

A four-person team built a taxonomy on "data domain × transfer strategy", cross-checked 130 references, and produced a 17-page IEEE two-column report with 9 figures and 6 tables.

Key takeaway: build the framework before filling in references; a survey is a map, not a list.
