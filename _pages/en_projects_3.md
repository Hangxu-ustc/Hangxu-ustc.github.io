A compact smart mirror integrating environmental sensing, information interaction and smart control — an exploration of embedded and IoT technology in smart-home scenarios.

- System integration and multi-layer PCB design around an STM32F4 MCU, with DHT11, HC-05 Bluetooth, a 1.8-inch TFT panel and a voice module.
- Offline voice interaction with 10+ core commands; recognition accuracy raised to about 95%; Wi-Fi module driven via AT commands to fetch real-time weather and time.
