A course project for Multimedia Communication. JPEG AI is the first end-to-end learned image compression standard — worth taking apart.

Started with a paragraph-by-paragraph reading report, then reproduced the hyperprior entropy model and rate–distortion loss in PyTorch, and finally compiled derivations, experiments and conclusions into a course report.

Reproducing once beats reading ten times: the role of side information in the hyperprior only became clear after running experiments.
